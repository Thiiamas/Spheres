# Lore — *Spheres* (working title)

> Intentionally **light**. The game is mostly abstract — round vs. angular. This
> doc exists only so level design, palette and enemy direction share one mental
> image. Don't over-write it.

## The eternal war

There are two worlds, and they have always been at war:

- The **sphere world** — round, smooth, whole. The player's consciousness lives
  here, jumping between allied spheres.
- The **cube world** — angular, hard-edged. Cubes (and other sharp shapes) press
  endlessly against the sphere world.

Neither side wins for good. The war is a tide, not a conquest.

## The Shore

The two worlds meet at a frontier called **the Shore** — the thin boundary
between round and angular. It behaves like a beach: the cube world **washes** in
across it like waves breaking onto sand, then pulls back. The spheres hold the
Shore.

A "wave" of enemies is meant literally — cubes **roll in along the cube-world
edge** as a tide line and advance across the Shore toward the sphere world. They
do **not** appear from a single point; they break across the whole frontier.

## What this means for the game

- **Orientation:** the sphere world is "home" (where spheres start and are
  defended); the cube world is the opposite edge the tide comes from.
- **Enemies spawn along the cube-world edge**, spread across its width, and move
  toward the spheres — a battle *line*, not a circle.
- **Tone:** abstract and clean. Lore is flavour, not exposition. No dialogue, no
  named characters — just round holding the line against angular.

> The "washing / tide" rhythm (waves that surge and recede, terrain that floods)
> is a future mechanic. For now only the **directional edge-spawn** expresses it.
